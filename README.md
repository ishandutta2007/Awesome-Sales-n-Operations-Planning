# Awesome-Sales-n-Operations-Planning

## Top Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Integrated Business Planning, Demand-Supply Balancing, Scenario Planning, Inventory Optimization & Cross-Functional Alignment*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP)** and related Integrated Business Planning (IBP). These systems align demand forecasts, supply plans, inventory targets, and financial goals through collaborative, scenario-based planning.



**Examples** include Kinaxis, Anaplan, Blue Yonder, OMP, Logility, ToolsGroup, RELEX Solutions, John Galt Solutions, Arkieva, and Oracle Supply Chain Planning (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise S&OP/IBP suites are almost entirely commercial. The strongest open option is **frePPLe** for demand forecasting and advanced planning & scheduling. Broader ERP/MRP open systems can support lighter planning processes. This section prioritizes these tools and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Kinaxis](https://www.kinaxis.com/)**  

  Concurrent planning platform (Maestro / RapidResponse) strong in real-time S&OP, supply planning, scenario evaluation, and cross-functional decision support.



- **[Anaplan](https://www.anaplan.com/)**  

  Connected planning platform widely used for S&OP and IBP, linking operational plans to financial outcomes with strong modeling flexibility.



- **[Blue Yonder](https://blueyonder.com/)**  

  AI-driven supply chain and S&OP platform with deep demand, supply, inventory, and fulfillment planning capabilities.



- **[OMP](https://www.omp.com/)**  

  Advanced planning and optimization suite particularly strong in process manufacturing, constraint-based supply planning, and end-to-end planning.



- **[Logility](https://www.logility.com/)**  

  Supply chain planning platform covering demand, supply, inventory, and S&OP with AI and analytics focus.



- **[ToolsGroup](https://www.toolsgroup.com/)**  

  Demand and inventory planning specialist with strong probabilistic forecasting and service-level optimization often used within S&OP processes.



- **[RELEX Solutions](https://www.relexsolutions.com/)**  

  Unified retail and supply chain planning platform strong in demand forecasting, replenishment, and collaborative planning.



- **[John Galt Solutions](https://johngalt.com/)**  

  Demand planning and S&OP software focused on forecasting accuracy and process support for mid-market and enterprise teams.



- **[Arkieva](https://arkieva.com/)**  

  Supply chain planning and S&OP solution emphasizing practical, usable planning for manufacturing and distribution.



- **[Oracle Supply Chain Planning](https://www.oracle.com/scm/)**  

  Oracle’s cloud supply chain planning suite covering demand, supply, S&OP, and related planning processes within the broader Oracle ecosystem.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[frePPLe](https://github.com/frePPLe/frepple)**  

  Leading open-source supply chain planning tool — demand forecasting, advanced planning & scheduling (APS), production planning, and inventory optimization. Community edition is MIT-licensed and practical for manufacturers moving beyond spreadsheets.



- **[Odoo MRP / Planning modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Open-source ERP with manufacturing, inventory, and planning capabilities that can support lighter S&OP-style processes when configured carefully.



- **[metasfresh](https://github.com/metasfresh/metasfresh)**  

  Open-source ERP with supply chain and operational planning features that can be extended for collaborative planning workflows.



- **[Opseron and unified open enterprise platforms](https://github.com/Opseron/Opseron)**  

  Emerging open platforms aiming to cover CRM, ERP, MRP, and operations in one system — potential foundation for integrated planning.



- **[Open optimization solvers (OR-Tools, OptaPlanner, PuLP, etc.)](https://github.com/)**  

  Constraint and optimization libraries used to build custom supply, capacity, or inventory planning engines.



- **[Time-series forecasting open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Prophet, statsmodels, NeuralProphet, and related tools used as the demand-planning foundation of custom S&OP processes.



- **[Spreadsheet + BI hybrid planning stacks](https://github.com/)**  

  Open approaches that combine governed data models, versioned spreadsheets, and dashboards for collaborative S&OP meetings.



- **[Inventory and replenishment open models](https://github.com/)**  

  Academic and community implementations of safety-stock, multi-echelon, and service-level optimization logic.



- **[Scenario and what-if simulation frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Open tools for running discrete-event or Monte-Carlo style scenarios around demand and supply variability.



- **[Data-integration open stacks for planning](https://github.com/)**  

  ETL and semantic-layer tools that prepare clean demand, inventory, and capacity data for any planning engine.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Starting with **frePPLe** when you need real APS and forecasting capability without a commercial license.

- Using Odoo or metasfresh as the transactional backbone and layering frePPLe or custom optimization on top.

- Building a lightweight S&OP process on open forecasting libraries + governed data + collaborative review tools.

- Accepting that concurrent planning, multi-enterprise visibility, advanced AI scenario engines, and polished cross-functional collaboration still favor commercial platforms.

- Focusing open efforts on demand forecasting accuracy and basic supply balancing before investing in full IBP suites.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Clean historical demand and inventory data → forecast with open time-series tools or frePPLe → balance supply and capacity with optimization solvers or frePPLe APS → review scenarios in collaborative dashboards → publish plans back to ERP. This stack works for mid-complexity manufacturing environments. Commercial platforms (Kinaxis, Anaplan, Blue Yonder, OMP, Logility, RELEX, Oracle, etc.) remain the practical choice for global, multi-echelon, financially integrated S&OP/IBP at scale.

## Disclaimer
